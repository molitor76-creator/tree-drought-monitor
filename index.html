Soil Water Trend

Estimated water stored in the modelled soil reservoir over time. The colour of each point indicates the drought-severity classification.

LOW At least 50% of assumed soil-water capacity remains.
MODERATE Water storage is between 25% and 50% of capacity.
HIGH Water storage is below 25% of capacity.
SEVERE Storage is below 25% for at least seven consecutive days.
WATER STORAGE The line value is estimated available water, measured in mm.
WATERING TARGET The model aims to restore storage to 60% of assumed capacity.
How to read the graph: Each data point represents the model's estimated soil-water storage on that date, in millimetres (mm). One millimetre is equivalent to one litre of water per square metre. The point colour shows the modelled drought severity. Hover over a point to see its exact storage, severity and consecutive stress days.

Important: These are model estimates, not direct soil-moisture measurements. The model assumes all rainfall enters the reservoir and uses reference evapotranspiration as a proxy for water loss. The soil capacities are provisional assumptions.
